Jo Podvin

Treading Spacetime

Hitchhike on a mote and float up through the light to slip clear of the clock and the list (float, not flight).

Unbending from gravity’s force gravitate towards the gap in the rift and the cleft in the break.

Into the effulgence of zero’s dark glow, where there’s something to swim in and nowhere to go.
