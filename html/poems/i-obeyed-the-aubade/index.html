i obeyed the aubade

i obeyed the aubade of the little birdsyes yessing the warmth, the light. iobeyed the aubade and dragged mybutt out from my dungeon of dudgeon,that my bones, too, might be hollowand resonant.

that i might puff up my feathers, addmy yes to the all.
