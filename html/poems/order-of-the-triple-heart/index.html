Order of the Triple Heart

Octopuses’eccentric arm-brainedtouchtasting lightshow proclivitiesinclude advanced degrees in shapeshiftingand slipsuckery. Playful curious—like us—yet not so gregarious. No society, so notendency toward hierarchy, rank, position;no notion of collective blood-spilling. No honor, so nohonor killing.
