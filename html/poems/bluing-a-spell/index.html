Bluing (A Spell)

Say ivory. Say alabaster. Say bone.Say bonemeal, girl! Say it! Sclera.Toilet paper. Dandelion puff. Cloud.Chemtrail. Gardenia. Skunk stripe.Say spun sugar lace collar snowflake.Say jasmine whipped cream cottonball, bridal veil waterfall cherryblossom. Say doily goose down angelwings.

SODIUM CHLORIDE. SAY IT.Rules the world.

Strand of pearls around the moon,around the milky chalky moon, themilky chalky larded moon.
