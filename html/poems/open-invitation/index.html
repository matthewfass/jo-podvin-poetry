Jo Podvin

open invitation

would you please explain to me this preference for gravitythis lingering upon the shore, legs plastered to the sand

when weightlessness awaits at hand, bright buoyancyabounds for free—leave lead behind, enter the sea

discover, know yourself to be mere tiny mote, bit ofdelight, held, embraced by liquid light—released

to play, awash in glee, loosened from sobrietyfloating, hyaline, vasty
