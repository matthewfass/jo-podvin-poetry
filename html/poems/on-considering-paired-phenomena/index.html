On Considering Paired Phenomena: The Increasing Infiltration of Dogs and Screens into All Aspects of Society

Dogs are specific. Dogs do not emit a wakeful bluish light. Life is a fiesta in the nose of a dog. Dogs are indifferent to satellites persevering overhead like sad comets. Dogs revel in wordlessness. Dogs laugh in their sleep. Dogs are textured. Dogs excel at Bhakti Yoga and enjoy a little Hatha Yoga, too. Dogs are enthusiasts. Dogs search themselves. Easily recharged, dogs need neither cord nor plug. Dogs consider anonymity out of the question. Dogs celebrate outdoors. Eschewing maps, dogs happily lead the way. When a dog pops up, it’s to say Let’s go! Dogs catch yawns as well as balls. While dog shit stinks, it’s not toxic waste. No one stands in line all night, eager for the chance to replace their old dog with the latest model.
