Gestation

No thing is born of the bright light of day.Scrape away the musty layer that liesbeneath the recently fallen leaves andthere, where rest the dead, the half-rotted, inthe realm of worms and sightless beings, you'llfind eggs and dreams about to hatch. No matchmust be lit before the darkness does its turn.The womb, fleshy urn, is wrapped in miles oftissue, cocooned in muscle, fat, and skin.Everything that is has worked its way outfrom within.
