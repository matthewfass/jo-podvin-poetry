ode to syringa vulgaris

for Liudwika, photographed as she grinned amongst lilacs

the first inhalation reaches back to the amygdalapleasantly the brain increasingly disorganizedwith each successive breath diffuses timeinto a heady haze of violet lavender pink-tingedwhite deep purple single-flowered double-floweredlacy tightly bunched erect

face buried intoxication lilac infusionof the unmediated euphoria this now inhabitingthe reptile in us sings altogether silentlythe amphibian stretches and smilesthe bird the bird does prettilywhat it is that birds do
